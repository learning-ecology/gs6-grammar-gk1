Answer Key & Explanations (Set 2)

Grade 6 English Syllabus Reference

Part 1: Multiple Choice

  • 1. A. do — Collocation: we say do judo, do karate, but play football.
  • 2. B. is waiting — "Hurry up!" and "right now" show an event taking place this moment (Present Continuous).
  • 3. C. opposite — Facing directly across the street = opposite.
  • 4. A. girls' — Plural regular noun (the girls) ending in -s takes only an apostrophe for possession.
  • 5. C. always — "very hardworking" implies high regularity (always finishes homework).
  • 6. B. is climbing — "Look at the kitten!" indicates action in progress.
  • 7. B. between — Followed by "and" (between the window and the wardrobe).
  • 8. B. Are they studying — Question form in Present Continuous signaled by "at present".

Part 2: Verb Tenses

  • 1. has — Routine daily schedule with 3rd-person singular "Phong".
  • 2. is knocking — "Listen carefully!" signals an immediate action.
  • 3. don't do / do not have — Negative routine with subject "We".
  • 4. is cooking — "Where is your mother? ... now" indicates ongoing action.
  • 5. Is Mai playing — Present Continuous yes/no question with singular subject "Mai".
  • 6. walks / is riding — Usual habit ("usually") contrasted with today's action ("today").

Part 3: Sentence Rewriting

  • 1. My brother is never late for school. (Adverbs of frequency come *after* the verb 'to be').
  • 2. The house is in front of the flower garden. (Opposite of 'behind').
  • 3. This is my teacher's computer. (Possessive 's applied to the owner).
  • 4. They usually do homework after dinner from Monday to Friday.
  • 5. The clock is on the wall.

Part 4: Error Correction

  • 1. Mistake: is usually having → Correction: usually has (daily routine uses Present Simple).
  • 2. Mistake: uncle's Tom → Correction: Uncle Tom's (the apostrophe belongs to the end of the full title/name).
  • 3. Mistake: in → Correction: on (things hang on a vertical wall surface).
  • 4. Mistake: sings → Correction: is singing ("Listen!" triggers Present Continuous).
